# BodyMetrics

**BodyMetrics** is a simple iOS and watchOS app to **log your weight**, **track BMI**, and **visualize progress over time**.

---

## Features

**Daily Weight Tracking**  
Easily record your weight with date selection.

**Automatic BMI Calculation**  
Instantly see your BMI and category.

**Interactive Charts**  
Visualize your weight trends over time.

**Apple Watch Companion App**  
Quickly view your progress right from your wrist.

**HealthKit Integration**  
Securely sync your weight data with Apple Health.

---

## Tech Stack

- **SwiftUI** – Declarative UI for iOS and watchOS
- **SwiftData** – Local data persistence
- **Charts** – Trend visualization
- **HealthKit** – Health data storage and syncing
- **WatchKit** – Watch app extension
